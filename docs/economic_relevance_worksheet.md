# Economic relevance annotation worksheet

**Created:** 2026-07-17 | **Status:** UNFILLED, working-session input

**Purpose.** Assign each concept an economic-relevance annotation on the four-level scale
(Very strong / Strong / Moderate / Thin). These annotations are the gating input to D6,
category and framework weighting (`docs/metric_methodology.md` par. 7). Verification on
2026-07-17 established that the systematic annotation was never previously completed;
this worksheet is where it happens. When filled, transcribe results into the master's
per-concept sections and lock D6.

**The question being answered, per concept:** how strong is the evidence linking this
governance concept to economic and development outcomes? This is about outcome linkage,
NOT measurement quality (a concept can be superbly measured yet thinly linked to outcomes,
and vice versa). Annotation informs weighting only; it never excludes a concept.

**Proposed level definitions (PROPOSAL, amend in session and record the final wording here):**
- **Very strong:** robust causal or quasi-experimental evidence, or overwhelming and consistent
  cross-country evidence, of a first-order channel to growth, investment, or development outcomes.
- **Strong:** consistent correlational evidence plus credible, well-understood mechanisms;
  mainstream-consensus linkage.
- **Moderate:** plausible mechanisms; evidence mixed, indirect, or context-dependent.
- **Thin:** weak or indirect evidence; relevance mainly normative or via long causal chains.

**Process notes:** record a 1-2 line rationale per concept. If the session cannot agree,
mark CONTESTED and note both views. Multi-placed concepts get ONE annotation (relevance is
a property of the concept, not the placement).

### 2. Political stability and regime durability

- **Category:** Political foundations
- **Scope:** durability of the political order, absence of unconstitutional change (coups, revolutions, mass political violence), absence of terrorism, low risk of large-scale political violence. Outcome-focused — what actually happens, not configuration of power (which is Political settlement).

### 3. Statistical and informational infrastructure

- **Category:** State capacity AND Accountability (vertical) — multi-placed
- **Scope:** capacity, integrity, and accessibility of official statistics. The standing data and information apparatus of the state — statistical agency independence and resources, statistical laws and methodology quality, and the actual public availability and timeliness of data. Multi-placed because it operates both as a state capability and as accountability infrastructure (citizens, journalists, opposition need data to evaluate government).
- **Primary (tier 1):** World Bank Statistical Performance Indicators (SPI); Open Data Inventory (ODIN, Open Data Watch)
